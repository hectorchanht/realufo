## Lore vs record (web research 2026-10-04)

- The Aug 1954 worldwide-saucers prophecy and the 1 Aug 1954 Ottawa radio test: found in no web retelling; documented only in this FBI file. New angle.
- Retellings (Wikipedia "Frances Swan", ufology.wikidot) say Eliot, Maine; the FBI says South Berwick (neighbouring towns). Knowles was a retired Rear Admiral.
- Affa and Ponnar names already appeared in G. H. Williamson's *The Saucers Speak* (1954).
- Earlier failed predictions (web): Affa to appear or broadcast 10 Jun 1954; world end "around 1956".
- The 1959 Washington episode (Cdr Julius Larsen in trance, window sighting, radar "blanked out", Maj. Robert Friend of Blue Book): rests on Friend's later account; Friend didn't see the UFO; no radar or CIA record found; the CIA history (Haines 1997) omits it. Not in our archive. Don't state as fact.
- Canada's Dept of Transport closed Project Magnet 10 Aug 1954, days after Smith's planned 1 Aug contact (web, mysteriesofcanada/Wikipedia). Not in our archive.
