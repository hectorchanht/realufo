A Hellfire missile hits a glowing orb off Yemen (MQ-9 video, 30 Oct 2024). Congress heard it "bounced right off" (p.32). Step through it: no flash at contact, and the missile keeps its line. It went through, not off.

If it really bounced: to throw a 48 kg Hellfire back and barely move, a 50–82 cm orb would need 4.8–8.5 tonnes. An elephant squeezed into a car tyre, still floating ~12,000 ft up. Our numbers: 1 px ≈ 4 cm at 5.7 km; missile 10.6 → 9.4 px/frame.
